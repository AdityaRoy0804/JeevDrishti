# Data sources

## Amur Tiger Re-identification (ATRW), via LILA BC
- Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial, share-alike, attribution required)
- Images owned by MakerCollider and the World Wildlife Fund
- Content: video-derived images of Amur tigers from Chinese zoos (captive animals, not wild camera traps)
- Citation: Li, S., Li, J., Lin, W., & Tang, H. (2019). Amur Tiger Re-identification in the Wild. arXiv:1906.05586
- Note: MegaDescriptor may have been trained on data that overlaps this dataset; results can look better than on new data.
